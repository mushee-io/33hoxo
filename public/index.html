Confidential execution for markets.

33HOXO lets applications seal trading intents before submission, reveal them only when the agreed Shutter trigger is satisfied, verify the original commitment, and route the verified intent into a settlement adapter.

Before reveal

Order side, action, size and price remain inside Shutter ciphertext.

At reveal

The threshold key unlocks the order and 33HOXO recomputes its commitment.

Settlement

Verified intents route into Mary Jane, auctions, DEXs, RFQs or other adapters.
